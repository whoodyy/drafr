Questions

Are towels included?

No. Towels are not included, and towel rental is ₱50 each.

How many guests can stay?

The rate is good for 20 pax, and the house sleeps up to 40. Excess pax is ₱500 per head on weekdays and ₱600 per head on weekends.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes, for a ₱1,000 fee per pet. Pets are not allowed on beds or furniture.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is parking space for 5 to 6 cars.

Is there WiFi?

Yes, WiFi is free.

Is there a pool for kids?

Yes. The pool has a 3 ft kiddie area, a 4 ft adult area, and free floaters.
